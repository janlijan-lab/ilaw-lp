1. Connect an AI (optional)

Google Gemini is recommended. Other providers are listed too, so pick the one you have a key for. You can also skip AI and use the template.

How does this work? (plain words)

An AI service is a company's computer that writes text when you send it a request. The API key is your personal password for that service. Most providers limit how many requests a key can make. This page sends requests straight from your browser to the provider; nothing is stored on any server. Keep learner names, grades, and personal details out of every AI tool.
